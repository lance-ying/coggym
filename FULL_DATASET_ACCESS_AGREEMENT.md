# CogGym 50-Study Dataset Research-Use Agreement

Version 1.1 — September 28, 2026

This agreement governs access to the nonpublic 50-study CogGym evaluation
dataset (the **Dataset**). The Dataset is separate from the 10-study public
subset in this repository.

## 2. Permitted use

The Dataset may be used only for noncommercial academic research involving
model evaluation, measurement, or scientific analysis. Access is personal to
the requester and members of the research group identified in the request. Any
additional user must separately accept this agreement.

## 3. Prohibited model training or optimization

The requester will not use the Dataset, in whole or in part, directly or
indirectly, to train, pretrain, fine-tune, align, preference-tune, perform
reinforcement learning, train a reward model, distill, perform retrieval-based
optimization, select prompts or hyperparameters against hidden answers,
generate training data, or otherwise update or optimize any model or system.

Running a frozen model to measure performance on the Dataset is permitted.
Evaluation outputs may not subsequently be used as training or optimization
data.

## 4. No commercial use

The Dataset may not be used for commercial purposes or for the development,
testing, promotion, or improvement of a commercial product or service. A
separate written agreement is required for any commercial use. To discuss
commercial access, contact [lanceying@mit.edu](mailto:lanceying@mit.edu).

## 5. No redistribution

The requester will not publish, upload, redistribute, sublicense, sell, lend,
or otherwise provide the Dataset or substantial portions of it to any third
party. Publications may report aggregate findings and limited illustrative
examples when doing so does not enable reconstruction of the Dataset.

## 6. Security and confidentiality

The requester will use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to
prevent unauthorized access. The Dataset may be stored only on access-
controlled institutional or personal research systems used by approved team
members. Any suspected loss or unauthorized disclosure must be reported
promptly to the CogGym team.

## 7. Human data

The requester will not attempt to identify or re-identify participants, link
records to external personal information, or contact participants. The
requester will comply with applicable institutional ethics and data-protection
requirements.

## 8. Attribution and publications

Publications and presentations using the Dataset must cite the CogGym paper
and the relevant original studies. The requester will accurately describe the
Dataset as a restricted evaluation set and will disclose the version used.

## 9. Third-party rights

Original-study materials remain subject to the rights and notices of their
respective owners. This agreement grants only the rights that the Dataset
provider is authorized to grant and does not supersede third-party terms.

## 10. Termination and deletion

Access may be terminated for breach of this agreement. Upon termination or a
written deletion request, the requester will delete all copies of the Dataset
and confirm deletion in writing. Results that do not contain or reconstruct
the Dataset may be retained for research-record purposes.

## 11. No warranty

The Dataset is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind. Access does not
imply endorsement of the requester's work or conclusions.

## 12. Electronic acceptance and access grant

By submitting the access form, typing a legal name, and affirmatively accepting
these terms, the requester electronically signs this agreement and represents
that the information in the request is accurate. In reliance on those
representations, CogGym grants the requester a personal, revocable,
non-transferable right to download and use the Dataset under this agreement.
Access begins when the download link is issued.
