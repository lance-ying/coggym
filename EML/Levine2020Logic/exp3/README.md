# Study 2b (Exp 3): Threshold vs no-threshold fishing scenario.

## Abstract
Participants evaluated a fishing-hook scenario in a Threshold or No-Threshold condition. They answered comprehension questions, a prediction question about others' beliefs, and moral/rule/outcome judgments.

## Overview
- Two conditions (threshold vs no-threshold).
- Comprehension and prediction questions precede moral judgments.
- Human data from `original_experiments/universalization/limitR2_v4.csv`.
