# Study 2a (Exp 2): Interest manipulation across contexts.

## Abstract
Participants read a single vignette (one of five contexts) in a High-Interest or Low-Interest condition and answered comprehension checks, moral permissibility, rule, and outcome questions.

## Overview
- 5 contexts (clams, fish, rabbits, birds, mushrooms) x 2 interest conditions.
- Each condition includes a comprehension page and three judgment pages.
- Human data from `original_experiments/universalization/contexts.csv`.
- Attention-check items described in the paper are excluded from the converted flow.
