# Aboody et al. (2025) Inferring Amorphous Knowledge.

## Abstract
People can infer the magnitude of another person's knowledge even when the specific contents of that knowledge are underdetermined. Aboody et al. introduce a computational account of graded epistemic inference and evaluate it in two experiments: (1) participants infer how much Sarah knows about prize locations from her field choices, and (2) participants jointly infer how much pirates know and how informative a map is based on their navigation choices.

## Overview
- Converted two experiments from the original Qualtrics materials and released participant data, with stimuli extracted from the Qualtrics PDF surveys.
- Experiment 1 uses two-field egg-hunt scenes; participants rate Sarah's knowledge for the left and right fields on each trial.
- Experiment 2 uses pirate island scenes; participants rate pirates' knowledge and perceived map informativeness.
- Inclusion trials, consent, and post-task questionnaires were excluded from the converted flows per the formatting specification.

## Directory Structure
- `exp1/`: Easter-egg hunt inference task with 19 trials.
- `exp2/`: Pirate-map inference task with 18 trials.
