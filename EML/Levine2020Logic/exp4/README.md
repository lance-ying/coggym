# Study 3 (Exp 4): Motor-oil scenarios and utility judgments.

## Abstract
Study 3 included two participant groups: a moral-judgment group and an expected-utility group. Both read motor-oil scenarios in tour-boat and fisherman contexts with high vs low interest and answered group-specific questions.

## Overview
- Moral-judgment group: comprehension, moral permissibility, rule, outcome.
- Expected-utility group: John's utility, fishermen's utility, frustrated means.
- Human data from `original_experiments/universalization/oil_moral.csv` and `motoroil5.csv`.
- The expected-utility story endings were reconstructed from the supplemental text; see conversion report for details.
