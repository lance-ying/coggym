# Hu et al. (2023) A fine-grained comparison of pragmatic language understanding in humans and language models

## Abstract
Hu et al. compare human and language-model pragmatic interpretation across seven phenomena using expert-curated multiple-choice items. Participants read short scenarios (or, in a follow-up, utterances without context) and select the best interpretation among distractors designed to capture literal and heuristic responses.

## Overview
- Converted the curated pragmatic materials from `lm-pragmatics/fj_raw_data`
  into EML for the contextual items in exp1.
- exp1 includes seven phenomena (Deceits, Indirect Speech, Irony, Maxims, Metaphor, Humour, Coherence Inference) with 170 total trials and human responses from 374 participants.
- The paper's no-context exp2 is not included because item-level human
  responses were not released.

## Directory Structure
- `exp1/`: Contextual pragmatic interpretation tasks with human response distributions.
