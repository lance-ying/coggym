# Bass et al. (2022) Partial mental simulation explains fallacies in physical reasoning.

## Abstract
Bass, Smith, Bonawitz, and Ullman investigate why people sometimes commit conjunction-fallacy-like errors in intuitive physical reasoning. Participants watch short animations of a gray cannonball and a pink sphere above a grassy field with a hole, then judge the likelihood of physical outcomes using 0-100 sliders. The study tests a partial mental simulation model against these judgments and shows strong correspondence between model predictions and human responses.
